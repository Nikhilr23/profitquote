Margin vs markup

They are not the same thing.

Gross margin is profit divided by revenue. Markup is profit divided by cost. If your true job cost is $237, adding a 35% markup gives $319.95 — but that is only about a 25.9% gross margin.

Minimum price for a target gross margin Price = Cost ÷ (1 − Margin)

$237 ÷ (1 − 0.35) = $364.62
